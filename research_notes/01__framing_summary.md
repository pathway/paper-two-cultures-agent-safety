# 01 — Framing summary (2026-11-20)

Source: Breiman (2001) main text [1] and `../framing.md`.

## Thesis
- Breiman's split: "data modeling" (assume the data are generated by the following model; validate by goodness-of-fit) vs "algorithmic modeling" (box unknown; validate by test-set accuracy).
- Agent safety has the same split. Objective-modeling culture: "assume the agent maximizes R", derive safety (CMDPs, shields, safe exploration). Behavior-modeling culture: treat the agent as a black box, measure what it does (evals, red teams, incidents), bound harm by architecture.
- McLean et al. [2] is the exhibit: it hypothesizes an "internalized" reward for a frozen LLM policy. That is "assume the data are generated by the following model" in RL dress.

## Why RL lands on Breiman's "statistics" side
- Mapping is by epistemic move, not community label. In robotics RL is the algorithmic culture vs control. The move that matters is: posit a parametric model of the inside of the box, reason from it as if it were the mechanism.
- Breiman's sentence transfers verbatim: "The conclusions are about the model's mechanism, and not about nature's mechanism" [1, §5]. Replace nature with the agent.

## Evidence plan (what makes it a paper)
1. Citation census to replace Breiman's guessed 98/2 (done, see 06).
2. Incident coverage count: fraction of real agent incidents a pre-deployment rule could have blocked (running).
3. Rashomon demo: one incident, three complete formal stories.
4. Transfer test from the debunking draft, generalized into an instrument.

## Where the analogy breaks
- Breiman had a shared scoreboard (test-set accuracy). Agent safety has none; both cultures are weak in the tail.
- Cox: "Often the prediction is under quite different conditions from the data." Every deployment is such a prediction.
- Efron: "Prediction by itself is only occasionally sufficient." A passed eval has no confidence interval over deployments.
- Hoadley: "score engineering" constraints on black-box scorecards = the hybrid to recommend.
- Evals are specifications too; they inherit Goodhart when the agent models the eval.
