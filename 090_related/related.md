No prior paper reads the RL primitives setting by setting for LLM systems, or sets the RL-agent culture against the LLM-agent culture. The nearest work comes in four strands. First, the formal reading of RLHF as a bandit: @ahmadian2024 argue that the whole generation should be modelled as a single action and the problem reduces to a bandit; @li2024 drop the value model for the same reason; @xiong2024 formalise RLHF as a KL-regularised contextual bandit; @lambert2025 contrasts the bandit and token-level MDP framings in a textbook. @rafailov2024 show that DPO nonetheless learns an implicit token-level Q-function. Second, the constrained reading of the KL penalty: @moskovitz2024 cast RLHF with several reward models as a constrained MDP with one constraint per reward model, and note that the KL coefficient already acts as a Lagrange multiplier, and @korbak2022 give the competing Bayesian reading. Third, the paper that states the equivalence: @mclean2026 "formalize the problem equivalence" [@mclean2026] between AI agents and RL agents and write the agent as a POMDP. On the reward primitive, @turner2022b argues, within RL, that a trained policy need not be a reward optimiser. Fourth, Breiman's frame: @breiman2001, and @damour2021 on the Rashomon set.
